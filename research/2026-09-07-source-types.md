## Classification rules

Use document purpose and form, not publisher or subject, to choose one primary type. Binding prudential instruments belong with laws and regulations. Treaties remain separate. Government policies and guidance share a category but retain scope and mandatory requirements in the details. Formal specifications belong with standards; implementation tooling belongs with testing and tools. Technical reports, taxonomies and crosswalks belong with research and databases, even when published by a standards body. Risk and control relationships remain unchanged.
