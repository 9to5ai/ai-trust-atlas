# Production use case methodology

Status: initial collection implemented for user review. **No monitoring schedule approved or created.**

## Admission

Require a named operator, a specific workflow, an original public account and an explicit statement of operating deployment. A launch announcement alone is insufficient if it only describes future availability. Prefer operator disclosures, annual reports and earnings material; supplier testimonials alone remain leads. Production, pilot, announced and retired are separate statuses. Default browsing includes production only.

Evidence authority is claim-specific: a company can report its deployment, but that does not independently establish performance, impact or effective safeguards. The first ten records are all labelled company-reported; none claims independent corroboration. Do not rank companies or infer maturity from brand size.

## Record and connect

Keep publication and review dates separate. Preserve a human-readable date when the precise day is unavailable; exclude it from rolling-day feeds rather than inventing a date. Record scope, AI actions, known human approval, missing details, reported outcomes and limitations. Targets and observational comparisons are not causal measured gains. Do not treat global employee/fleet counts as verified adoption of a specific system.

Deduplicate by operator + workflow, updating an existing record for material changes. Preserve history and source dates when refreshing. Keep discontinued deployments as explicitly retired records, outside the default production collection. Connections to concepts and controls require a specific decision mechanism and an authored reason. They are questions to examine, not claims that the company uses the control. No automatic incident links are inferred from a shared topic or brand: add only an individually explained mechanism link, without implying an incident affected this deployment.
